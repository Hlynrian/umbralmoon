Project Note

First Light Under the Umbral Moon

The first marker in the road: a note from before Umbral Moon had fully declared what kind of project it wanted to become.

The Setting Campaign

Umbral Moon began as a place to gather tabletop work: campaign notes, battle reports, maps, rules, miniatures, reviews, and whatever else accumulated around the table. As the setting developed, those separate interests began to point in the same direction.

The project is now centered on Umbral Moon itself: a science-fiction campaign in which battlefield actions, smaller operations, investigation, politics, worldbuilding, and the physical work of making the game all contribute to one developing setting. This site is the journal of that process.

Umbral Moon is meant to hold writing about old-school tabletop games with atmosphere, patience, and a taste for the mythic. Reviews can live here, but so can campaign notes, battle reports, maps, rules talk, miniatures, and reflections from the table.

The site begins simply: static files, quiet structure, a lunar visual language, and no machinery beyond what the writing needs.

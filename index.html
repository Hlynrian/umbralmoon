The Shape of the Site

Umbral Moon should feel like a journal kept near a dying fire: legible, patient, a little severe, and built for essays that take games seriously without sanding away their mystery.

The visual direction begins with lunar contrast, aged paper warmth, bone-white type, and sparing ember accents.

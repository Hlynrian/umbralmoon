The Shape of the Site

Umbral Moon should feel like a campaign journal kept beside dice, maps, painted figures, old modules, and notes from the table: legible, patient, and built for play that still leaves room for mystery.

The visual direction begins with lunar contrast, aged paper warmth, bone-white type, and sparing ember accents, while the heart of the site stays with old-school adventure.

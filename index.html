The Setting Campaign

The Project

One Setting, Several Scales

Umbral Moon is built as a campaign first. A full battlefield action, a seven-person retinue duel, a covert raid, an investigation, or a political dispute can all matter to the same developing situation. The rules can change with the scale of the action; the campaign provides the continuity.

The setting begins on the fringe, where local institutions have learned to survive with little help from the distant Empire. Its authority is real, but its attention is rare. Most problems must be solved by the people already here, before they become large enough to attract notice from farther inward.

Umbral Moon

Open war and hidden war belong to the same campaign. Armies fight for ground while smaller groups investigate, raid, bargain, recover prisoners, conceal evidence, and uncover the reasons the larger battles are being fought.

The Project

One Campaign, Several Scales

Umbral Moon is a tabletop campaign setting built to move naturally between different scales of play. A battle between military formations, a raid by a handful of operatives, an investigation inside a fortress, or an archaic seven-against-seven retinue duel can all change the same situation. The rules may change with the kind of action taking place; the campaign is what holds it together.

The setting begins far from the center of an old Empire, where local institutions have learned to handle most problems themselves. The Empire is distant, and most people prefer it that way. If something happens that makes the Empire pay attention, the situation has probably already become serious.
